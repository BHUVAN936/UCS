ABOUT THE PROJECT

One interface for university information.

UCE Connect is a Flask-based university information portal designed to make academic, placement, counselling, examination, faculty, research, international and institutional information easier to discover.

01
THE GOAL

Make information understandable.

Instead of presenting users with disconnected documents and long pages, UCE Connect organizes information into focused modules and subcategories.

02
THE APPROACH

Separate information from presentation.

The interface remains consistent while university information is organized according to its corresponding module and category.

03
THE STRUCTURE

Organize information by purpose.

University information is divided into twenty focused modules covering academics, placements, counselling, examinations, faculty, research, international programs, student development and institutional facilities.

04
THE SYSTEM

Let uploaded information drive the portal.

Administrators can upload university data and associate it with the appropriate module and category without changing the portal interface.
